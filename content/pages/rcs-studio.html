Common Questions

What is the current RCS device coverage?

RCS is available on Android devices using Google Messages and iOS 18+. Combined coverage exceeds 1.2 billion devices globally.

How does pricing work?

Usage-based pricing you pay per message sent. RCS Basic and RCS Multimedia have different per-message rates. Volume discounts available. Contact us for a custom quote.

What happens when a device doesn't support RCS?

Automatic fallback to SMS. Signalmash detects device capabilities and routes accordingly. You can customize fallback content.

How long does brand verification take?

2-5 business days. Signalmash manages the carrier submission process on your behalf.

Can RCS replace our mobile app?

For many use cases, yes. RCS can handle product catalogs, order tracking, appointment booking, customer support, and more — all without requiring users to download an app.

Can I migrate existing SMS campaigns to RCS?

Yes. Start by adding verified sender branding, then introduce rich media and interactive elements incrementally.
