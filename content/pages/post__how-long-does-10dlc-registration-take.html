How Long Does A2P 10DLC Registration Take? Realistic Timelines (2026)

Quick answer: how long A2P 10DLC registration takes

A complete, internally consistent 10DLC submission is normally finished inside one week: brand registration clears in minutes to 24 hours, standard vetting returns a trust score in one to three business days, and standard campaign review takes one to three business days. A rejection does not cost you a day; it costs a full review cycle plus the resubmission, which in practice adds one to two weeks. Special use cases such as political, charity, or agents-and-franchises campaigns need extra documentation and run one to three weeks even when everything is correct. The single biggest factor in your timeline is whether the submission is right first time.

The timeline, stage by stage

Day 0: gathering what you need

This stage is where most of the calendar time is actually lost, and it is entirely in your hands. You need the exact legal entity name and EIN as they appear on the IRS letter, the registered business address from your state filing, a live website on your own domain with a privacy policy that says mobile numbers are not shared with third parties for marketing, a documented opt-in flow you can screenshot, and two to five sample messages with brand name and opt-out language. Businesses that have these ready register in an afternoon; businesses that discover the privacy policy needs a sentence added, or that the website is still a coming-soon page, lose days before anything is submitted. The A2P 10DLC requirements checklist lists all six items.

Brand registration: minutes to 24 hours

Once submitted, the brand record is checked against IRS and state records. When the legal name, EIN and address match, the brand is usually created within minutes and almost always within a day. When they do not match, the brand sits in an unverified state until it is corrected, and every day spent there pushes everything else back.

Vetting: one to three business days

Standard third-party vetting, the normal choice, usually returns a trust score within one to three business days and sometimes the same day. Enhanced vetting takes a similar time once its supporting documents are in. Skipping vetting saves that wait but leaves you on conservative default throughput, which for most businesses is a false economy. See throughput and trust scores.

Campaign review: one to three business days

A standard campaign (customer care, notifications, 2FA, marketing, mixed) is reviewed for consistency: does the use case match the samples, is the opt-in real and single-brand, are STOP and HELP handled, is the website legitimate. Clean submissions clear in one to three business days. The campaign approval checklist is what reviewers are effectively working through.

Number attachment and first send: same day

Once the campaign is approved, attaching numbers and sending the first message is a same-day task. Do attach numbers promptly: T-Mobile charges a non-use fee on campaigns left without numbers.

What a rejection adds

A rejection restarts the campaign review clock. You fix the issue, resubmit, pay a campaign vetting fee again with most providers, and wait another one to three business days for review. In practice, with the back-and-forth of reading the rejection reason, correcting a privacy policy or re-shooting an opt-in screenshot, and resubmitting, a rejection typically adds one to two weeks. The most common reasons are a use-case mismatch (a promotional sample in a transactional campaign), missing opt-out language in the samples, an opt-in that cannot be evidenced, a privacy policy without the third-party sentence, and a website that does not resolve or does not match the brand's domain. Our post on why 10DLC campaigns get rejected covers each with the fix.

Special use cases: one to three weeks

Political, charity, emergency, agents-and-franchises and similar special campaigns require additional external vetting and documentation, and their reviews are slower and stricter. Plan on one to three weeks from a correct submission, and do not schedule a send date that depends on approval landing on a specific day.

How to finish inside a week

  1. Run the checks before you submit. The free 10DLC readiness checker asks the 22 questions a reviewer would and tells you what to fix first.
  2. Copy the legal name from the EIN letter, not from your website or your bank.
  3. Add the privacy-policy sentence about not sharing mobile numbers with third parties, and link the policy from the opt-in form, before you register.
  4. Order standard vetting with the brand rather than after, so the score is back by the time the campaign is reviewed.
  5. Register the right use case. If you send both promotions and transactional messages, register mixed rather than stretching a transactional campaign.
  6. Have someone who has done it before submit it. Signalmash registers brands and campaigns for customers as part of onboarding and handles any resubmission, which is the fastest route for a team without a compliance specialist.

People also ask

How long does 10DLC brand registration take?

When the legal name, EIN and address match IRS and state records, brand registration usually completes within minutes and almost always within 24 hours. Mismatches leave the brand unverified until corrected, which is the most common cause of a slow start.

How long does 10DLC campaign approval take?

Standard campaigns are typically reviewed in one to three business days from a complete submission. Special use cases such as political or charity campaigns take one to three weeks because they require extra vetting and documentation.

How long does 10DLC vetting take?

Standard vetting usually returns a trust score in one to three business days, sometimes the same day. Enhanced vetting and appeals take a similar time once the supporting documents have been submitted.

Can 10DLC registration be expedited?

Expedited campaign vetting is offered by the registry at a premium and shortens the campaign review, but it cannot fix a submission that would be rejected. The fastest reliable route is a correct first submission: matching legal details, an evidenced opt-in, compliant samples and the right use case.

Why is my 10DLC registration taking so long?

The usual causes are a brand stuck in an unverified state because the legal name or address does not match public records, a campaign rejected for a use-case mismatch or missing opt-out language, or a special use case that requires external vetting. Check the status of the brand first; if it is verified and the campaign is pending beyond three business days, ask your provider for the reviewer's notes.
