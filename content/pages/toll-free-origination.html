What is Toll-Free Origination?

Toll-Free Origination routes inbound calls through a verified toll-free number (8XX) directly into your SIP network. This provides a professional, nationwide presence and ensures customers across the U.S. and Canada can connect with your business quickly and at no cost to them.

With Signalmash, you get enhanced call quality, reduced latency, and flexible routing options, all at competitive rates designed for businesses of any size.

What Is a Local Number?

A local number is a geographically assigned phone number in the U.S. or Canada that helps your business establish a local identity. Even if your team operates remotely, customers feel they’re calling a nearby business building trust, familiarity, and stronger engagement.
