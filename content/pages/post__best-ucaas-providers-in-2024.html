Best UCaaS Providers in 2026

Quick answer: the best UCaaS providers for business

The most widely chosen UCaaS (unified communications as a service) providers are Zoom, RingCentral, Nextiva, 8x8 and Webex by Cisco, which bundle calling, video meetings, messaging and contact-center features into one cloud subscription. Signalmash sits alongside them as the carrier layer: elastic SIP trunking, phone numbers and Branded Caller ID that plug into any UCaaS or PBX at per-minute rates, plus MashPBX for businesses that want a hosted phone system with CPaaS APIs underneath. The comparison below covers pricing, features and best-fit use cases.

Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) is one of the modern leaps, helping businesses transform and revolutionize their communication practices. UCaaS is a cloud-based solution that brings different modes of communication, including text, calls, and video conferencing, to a single place.

Several UCaaS providers are offering their solutions to businesses. However, it is often a challenge to pick the one that meets the needs of your business. In this article, we introduce some of the best UCaaS providers and their distinguishing features.

2. Zoom

Cost: Starting at USD 27 per month per user

Zoom has developed as a strong Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS) provider over the last few years. Zoom initially started as a video-conferencing tool but has now developed into a comprehensive platform, integrating various communication tools such as messaging, file sharing, and voice calls.

One of the best aspects of Zoom is its scalability which allows organizations to adjust their communication needs. Zoom’s UCaaS offers robust features, and reliable communication, and ensures effective collaboration with the team, making it a good choice for modern organizations.

Some unique features of Zoom include the following.

    • Easy and user-friendly interface, making the platform familiar and simple to use.
    • Option to create and host webinars, along with audio and video meetings.
    • Unlimited phone calls and the ability to collaborate with teammates.
    • Live transcription and recording options allow easy access to notes and better accessibility.

3. RingCentral

Cost: Starting at USD 20 per month per user

RingCentral is another leading UCaaS provider, integrating voice, video, messaging, and team collaboration into a single cloud-based solution. RingCentral has been the leader in UCaaS services for over 5 years, making it one of the key players in the market.

One of the key aspects that sets RingCentral apart is its global presence and remarkable infrastructure. The global footprint of RingCentral allows businesses to connect with collaborators and clients in over 100 countries, providing a consistent experience worldwide.

Some unique features of RingCentral include the following.

  • Video conferencing and improved teamwork with RingCentral Glip, providing several functionalities such as file sharing, messaging, and collaboration.
  • Enabling the customers to collaborate with the help of a dedicated digital customer engagement platform.
  • The opportunity and affordance to create custom integrations and applications on top of the ones already provided by RingCentral gives more flexibility to the users.

4. Nextiva

Cost: Starting at USD 19 per month per user

Nextiva is one of the leading UCaaS providers in the market, offering a wide range of services such as audio and video calls, customer relationship management (CRM) solutions, and messaging. Nextiva is best known for its comprehensive communication suite that makes it easy for organizations to understand the trends and make data-driven decisions.

The important aspects of Nextiva include its focus on security, reliability, and customer satisfaction. The UCaaS platform provides personalized support along with unique features, making it a strong contender in the market.

Some unique features of Nextiva include the following.

  • Integration of all communication channels into a single interface with the help of the NextOS platform.
  • Understanding the communication patterns and trends with the help of AI analytics.
  • Integrations with several third-party apps, including CRM integrations and productivity tools.

5. 8×8

Cost: Starting at USD 24 per month per user

8×8 has been constantly rated as one of the best UCaaS providers over the years. With its reliable service and utility of multiple channels (including audio calls, emails, chats, and video conferencing) 8×8 ensures efficiency in a single platform. One of the best aspects of 8×8 is its consistent and personalized customer experience in all interactions.

Similar to other leading UCaaS platforms, 8×8 also offers integrations with several applications, such as Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, and many others.

Some of the most prominent features of 8×8 include the following.

  • Conversation insights with automatic transcription of communication can help in conducting important analyses.
  • Integration of AI into communication solutions by offering virtual agents that can help in automating tasks.
  • Visualization and categorization of important keywords that can help in highlighting customer and employee sentiments in an interaction.

6. Webex by Cisco

Cost: Starting at USD 25 per month per user

Webex is one of the most reliable platforms when it comes to UCaaS. With the trusted name of Cisco attached to it, Webex has quickly gained the trust of businesses. Many leading organizations are using this UCaaS provider, making Webex popular and earning the status of a leader from Gartner.

Webex comprehensively employs AI, making it a good choice for different businesses in the modern world. Several healthcare, financial, and public sector organizations are using Webex to better manage their internal and external communication.

Some of the distinguishing features of Webex are as follows.

  • Easy organization of web conferences and webinars, making it a popular choice for businesses relying on digital platforms.
  • Easy-to-use interface with familiar designs on different devices and platforms, making it an accessible UCaaS provider.
  • Smart dashboard in the form of a Control Hub to manage all devices and communication activities.
