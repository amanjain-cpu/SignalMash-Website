Frequently Asked Questions

Is Branded Caller ID the same as CNAM?

No. CNAM typically shows a 15-character name. Branded Caller ID extends this with verified branding—your logo, company name, and even a call reason.

Can I choose any name for outbound CNAM?

There are compliance rules and character limits. Typically, you can display your legal name or DBA (Doing Business As). SignalMash guides you through setup for accuracy.

Do inbound CNAM queries work internationally?

Inbound CNAM is mainly supported in the U.S., with some availability in Canada. International coverage varies by carrier and region.

How often do carriers update CNAM databases?

Updates usually take a few days, but timing depends on the carrier. Once you set up CNAM with Signalmash, we handle synchronization across major databases.

Do I need special hardware to enable CNAM or Branded Caller ID?

No. Caller branding happens at the network level with Signalmash. No additional equipment is required.

Do Branded Caller ID and CNAM have the same reach?

No. CNAM is only visible to handset users who subscribe to caller ID services, mostly on landlines. Branded Caller ID is broader: it is supported on iOS, Android and Samsung devices across T-Mobile, Verizon and AT&T, and reaches the majority of US mobile subscribers.

How do I stop my business calls being marked Scam Likely?

Register your numbers with the carrier analytics providers, keep calling patterns consistent, use STIR/SHAKEN A-level attestation and monitor your number reputation. Signalmash's Number Intelligence service registers and remediates flagged numbers for you.

How long does Branded Caller ID take to set up?

Business vetting and number registration typically take 5-10 business days. After approval your name and logo appear on supported iPhone and Android devices on all major US carriers, and changes to branding are usually reflected within 24-48 hours.

What is Branded Caller ID?

Branded Caller ID (BCID) displays your verified business name, logo and call reason on the recipient's phone for outbound calls, backed by STIR/SHAKEN authentication. It replaces plain CNAM text and typically lifts answer rates by 30-40%.

What is CNAM and how is it different from Branded Caller ID?

CNAM is the 15-character caller name stored in carrier databases and shown on landlines and some mobile phones. Branded Caller ID is a newer, verified display that adds your logo and call reason on mobile devices. Signalmash provisions both from one dashboard.
