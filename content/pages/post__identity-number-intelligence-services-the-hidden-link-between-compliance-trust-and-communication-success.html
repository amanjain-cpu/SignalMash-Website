What Are Identity & Number Intelligence Services?

Identity and Number Intelligence Services combine essential tools that verify phone numbers, authenticate users, and ensure your communications meet compliance standards. These include:

  • Carrier Lookup: Instantly identify the carrier, local routing number (LRN), and whether a number is mobile or landline.

  • Fed DNC Lookup: Check if a number has been added to the federal Do Not Call (DNC) list in the past 30 days helping you avoid violations.

  • Inbound CNAM Query: View caller details before you answer. Ideal for screening unwanted or fraudulent calls.

  • Inbound CNAM: Display the caller’s name on your Signalmash phone numbers for all inbound calls.

  • Outbound CNAM Listing: Present a verified business name when calling recipients in the US or Canada.

  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Send one-time passwords via SMS or voice for secure logins, transactions, and account updates.

Together, these services form a secure foundation for any business that depends on communication reliability and data integrity.

Why Identity & Number Intelligence Services Matter

1. Improve Routing Accuracy and Call Efficiency

Carrier Lookup eliminates wasted calls and misrouted connections by identifying the correct network. The result: higher delivery rates, lower costs, and stronger engagement across voice and SMS channels.

2. Maintain Compliance and Avoid Penalties

Fed DNC Lookup helps your outreach remain fully compliant with federal Do Not Call rules. This proactive filtering shields your organization from costly fines and compliance risks.

3. Strengthen Brand Trust

With CNAM services, your business appears as a verified and recognizable name on outbound calls. Customers are more likely to answer, and your team gains clarity on inbound calls—reducing fraud risk and improving service response.

4. Secure Customer Interactions

2FA provides an extra layer of protection against unauthorized access. Whether for user logins, transactions, or sensitive updates, it’s a straightforward safeguard for both your customers and your business.

5. Simplify Management with an Integrated Platform

Managing multiple providers slows teams down. Signalmash brings identity verification, compliance checks, and authentication under one platform—reducing overhead and improving operational visibility.
