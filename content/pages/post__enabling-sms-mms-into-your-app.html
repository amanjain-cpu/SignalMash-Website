What Is CPaaS, you ask?

CPaaS stands for “communications platform as a service.” CPaaS providers like Signalmash and Twilio offer functionality into your own app. This includes messaging, text notifications, and voice call automation. Companies that offer CPaaS solutions provide a communications API so you can incorporate communications functionality into your own software or application. This means you don’t have to build your own communications platform and infrastructure. You rely on the CPaaS to do that for you, which saves a lot of time and money.
