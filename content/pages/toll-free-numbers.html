What is a Toll-Free Number?

A toll-free number is a business phone number with prefixes like 800, 888, 877, 866, 855, 844, or 833 that lets customers call your business without paying long-distance charges.

These numbers create a professional, nationwide presence while making it easy for customers to connect with you.
