What is a Short-Code Number?

Short codes are 5- to 6-digit numbers built for high-volume text messaging. Unlike standard phone numbers, short codes are reserved for business SMS and MMS, giving you:

  • Fast message delivery for time-sensitive campaigns.

  • Stronger deliverability with carrier-approved sending.

  • Built-in compliance with wireless carrier and CTIA standards.

Dedicated Short-Codes

A dedicated short-code belongs exclusively to your business, so you control every interaction. Businesses use them for:

  • Mass alerts and notifications.

  • Authentication and security codes

  • Marketing campaigns at national scale.
