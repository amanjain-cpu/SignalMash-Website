FAQ

What is Compliance as a Service?

It is a managed framework that handles all regulatory and carrier requirements for SMS, MMS, and voice campaigns so businesses can send messages without risk of blocks or fines.

Who is Signalmash Compliance as a Service designed for?

Resellers, ISVs, affiliates, and communication providers who want reliable compliance coverage without building internal teams or systems.

Does Signalmash manage 10DLC registration?

Yes. We handle brand registration, campaign approvals, content validation, and ongoing monitoring to ensure every 10DLC route stays compliant.

How does CaaS help prevent carrier filtering or campaign suspensions?

All campaigns are reviewed and validated before launch. This reduces errors, avoids policy violations, and improves long term deliverability.

Can Signalmash manage toll free and short code approvals?

Yes. We coordinate full toll free verification and short code application workflows to avoid long delays and repeated rejections.

What compliance areas are included in the COaaS Suite?

Compliance, billing, taxation, KYC, customer support, and collections. These services work together to support operational growth.

What revenue share models are available?

Most partners participate in a negotiated bi-lateral model that ranges from 5 to 20 percent depending on volume and service scope.

How quickly can new customers be onboarded?

Onboarding times vary by use case, but compliance automation and expert review help accelerate registrations and reduce waiting periods.
