What Is a Local Number?

A local number is a geographically assigned phone number in the U.S. or Canada that helps your business establish a local identity. Even if your team operates remotely, customers feel they’re calling a nearby business building trust, familiarity, and stronger engagement.

SMS & MMS messaging FAQs

What is an SMS API?

An SMS API lets your application send and receive text messages programmatically over carrier networks. Signalmash's REST API supports SMS and MMS on 10DLC, toll-free and short code numbers, with delivery receipts, inbound webhooks and compliance handling built in.

How much does business SMS cost with Signalmash?

Signalmash prices SMS on a single all-inclusive rate with no hidden carrier surcharges, and local 10DLC numbers start at $0.50 per month. Current per-message rates for SMS, MMS and RCS are listed on the pricing page, with volume discounts for high-volume senders.

Do I need 10DLC registration to send business texts?

Yes. US carriers require any business sending application-to-person SMS from a 10-digit local number to register its brand and campaign with The Campaign Registry. Signalmash completes the registration for you, typically within a few business days.

Which number type should I use: 10DLC, toll-free or short code?

Use 10DLC for conversational and moderate-volume messaging from a local number, toll-free for nationwide notifications without campaign registration, and a short code when you need the highest throughput and deliverability for OTPs, alerts or mass marketing.
