What is a Signalmash Query?

Signalmash Query is a real-time phone number lookup service that helps your business work smarter and stay compliant. With a single API call or portal search, you can instantly access critical insights about any phone number:

Subscriber Info (CNAM)

Identify the person or business behind a number to personalize calls.

Carrier Information

Confirm the current carrier to improve routing.

Line Type (Mobile or Landline)

Distinguish between wireless and fixed lines for accurate messaging and cost control.

Federal DNC Status

Instantly check Do Not Call registry status to reduce compliance risk.

Why it matters: Fewer misdials, better customer targeting, optimized call routing, and reduced risk of regulatory fines.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly does the Signalmash Query return results?

In most cases, queries are returned in real time—often within milliseconds—ensuring minimal delays in your workflow.

Can I integrate Query with my CRM or dialer?

Absolutely. We offer a RESTful API and detailed documentation so you can seamlessly integrate lookups into your existing systems

Does it support international numbers?

Yes, although data quality and available information may vary by region. Contact our team for specifics on international coverage.

Does querying the DNC status require any special compliance steps?

We handle DNC checks on our end, but you’re responsible for respecting opt-outs and adhering to regulations. Our KYC process ensures you meet core telecom guidelines.

Is subscriber information (CNAM) always accurate?

We source CNAM data from reputable carrier databases, but accuracy can vary based on how frequently carriers update their records.
