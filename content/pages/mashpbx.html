What Is MashPBX?

MashPBX is a cloud-based PBX (Private Branch Exchange) built for businesses that need dependable and flexible communications. From small companies to enterprise teams, MashPBX delivers advanced calling features, simple management, and seamless integrations—all backed by Signalmash’s secure, geo-redundant infrastructure.
