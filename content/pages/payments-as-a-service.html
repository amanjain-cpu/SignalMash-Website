Collections as a Service

Signalmash will assist in any collections and payment recovery for partners, including recurring invoices, overdue notifications, and failed payment handling. The system tracks every transaction, provides revenue analytics, and ensures funds flow cleanly from customer to partner — reducing administrative overhead and cashflow gaps.

To help partners maintain steady revenue by handling collections when it, freeing teams from chasing payments and allowing them to focus on growth. Signalmash Collections as a Service is part of the COaaS Suite, which is a complete operational framework for communications providers, ISVs, and affiliates that want to expand their offering, streamline logistics, and unlock new revenue opportunities — without building internal infrastructure from scratch.

Through a negotiated bi-lateral revenue share model (typically 5–20%), Signalmash becomes the operational layer that powers your business — automating compliance, billing, taxation, KYC, customer support, and collections across all messaging and voice services.

Partners bring their complementary technology or customer base; Signalmash delivers the backbone that keeps everything compliant, billable, and sustainable.
