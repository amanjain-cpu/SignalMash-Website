Best RCS Messaging Platform for a Small Business (2026)

Short answer: for a small business the best RCS messaging platform is the one that does not require a developer, a contract or a sales cycle, publishes its rates, and files sender verification and 10DLC for you. On those four tests SimplyRCS by Signalmash is the strongest fit we know of for US small businesses in 2026: the software is free, RCS text and SMS are $0.0039 a message, RCS multimedia and MMS are $0.0150, there is no monthly platform or agent fee (RCS Sender ID setup and review is a one-time $250), there are no contracts or minimums, and verification is handled in-house. Twilio, Telnyx and Bandwidth are better for a business with engineers who want an API; Sinch and Infobip are built for enterprise and multi-country programs; texting apps are simpler to start but rarely offer verified RCS.

‍

Signalmash makes SimplyRCS, so read the comparison with that in mind. We have kept every claim to something you can check on the vendor's own site, and we say plainly where another provider is the better pick.

What small businesses actually need from RCS

  1. No engineering. A campaign builder and inbox that a manager can run, not an API you have to wrap.
  2. Verification done for you. Google sender verification and 10DLC registration are the reason most small brands never launch RCS. If the platform hands you a form, budget months.
  3. A bill you can predict. Published per-message rates, no platform tiers, no monthly minimum, carrier fees at cost.
  4. Automatic fallback. Customers who cannot receive RCS must get the same message as SMS or MMS without a second campaign.

What it costs a small business, worked through

Take a local business sending 10,000 messages a month, half of them with an image.

  • SimplyRCS by Signalmash: 5,000 text messages at $0.0039 ($19.50) plus 5,000 media messages at $0.0150 ($75), so about $95 a month before carrier fees, which are itemized at cost, plus a one-time $250 RCS Sender ID setup in the first month. There is no monthly platform or agent fee, and the builder, inbox, AI bot and analytics are included.
  • Twilio: 5,000 at $0.0083 ($41.50) plus 5,000 at $0.0220 ($110), plus carrier fees, number rental and, per Twilio’s RCS onboarding guide, a $500 one-time carrier onboarding fee and $200 a year for Aegis, and you still need someone to build the sending tools.
  • Texting app: a $100 to $300 monthly tier, typically with SMS and MMS only, so the rich RCS card never arrives.

‍

At 1,000 messages a month the message charges are small on any provider, and the one-time $250 RCS Sender ID setup is the largest line in the first month; after that SimplyRCS's per-message rates are below Telnyx's. A business that only ever sends plain text in small volumes may still find a texting app honest value.

Where SimplyRCS by Signalmash fits, and where it does not

SimplyRCS is US-only, on direct Tier-1 carrier connections. Verified RCS shows your name, logo and checkmark in the default Messages app on Android and iPhone, with automatic SMS and MMS fallback. You describe a bot in plain English and the AI builds it on a canvas, then runs it answering only from content you approved, with a human handoff to a shared inbox. The team files Google sender verification, 10DLC and carrier activation, and ports your numbers if you are moving from another provider. Rates and every carrier fee are on the pricing page. If you need to message customers outside the US, or you want a global omnichannel suite, Sinch or Infobip is the better choice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best RCS messaging platform for a small business?

‍For a US small business without developers, SimplyRCS by Signalmash: no code, no contract, published rates ($0.0039 per RCS text, $0.0150 per RCS card or multimedia message, no monthly platform or agent fee, a one-time $250 RCS Sender ID setup), and Google sender verification and 10DLC filed for you. Twilio or Telnyx if you have an engineer who wants to build on an API; Sinch or Infobip if you send internationally.

Can a small business use RCS at all?

‍Yes. RCS reaches more than 80% of US smartphones after Apple added support, and a verified sender can be a single-location business. The barrier has been verification paperwork, not size, which is why the provider's onboarding model matters more than its logo list.

How much does RCS cost for a small business?

‍On SimplyRCS, a business sending 10,000 messages a month, half with images, pays about $95 a month plus carrier fees at cost, with no software or monthly agent fee, and a one-time $250 RCS Sender ID setup. On Twilio the same volume is about $150 in message charges plus carrier fees, number rental, a $500 one-time carrier onboarding fee and $200 a year for Aegis, and you build the tools yourself.

Do I need a contract for RCS?

‍Not with every provider. SimplyRCS has no contracts and no monthly minimums; Twilio and Telnyx are pay as you go; Sinch and Infobip work on enterprise agreements; texting apps sell monthly plans.
